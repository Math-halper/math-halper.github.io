# Web Retro's bundled ROM library

Web Retro, in the Tools section of the proton menu, comes with 34 bundled retro games: the site maintainer's own ROM files. To play one, open Web Retro, stay on the ROMs tab, click a console box (NES, SNES, N64 or GB) and click a game. There is no start button: the game boots straight away. The games are commercial titles that belong to Nintendo and their other rights holders.

## Nintendo 64 (8)

Mario Kart 64, Mario Party, Paper Mario, Doom 64, Yoshi's Story, Super Mario 64, Banjo-Kazooie and Super Smash Bros.

## NES (9)

Excitebike, Super Mario Bros. + Duck Hunt, Super Mario Bros. 2, Super Mario Bros. 3, Tetris, Castlevania, Contra, Mega Man 2 and Metroid.

## SNES (5)

Super Mario World, The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past, Donkey Kong Country, Super Metroid and Chrono Trigger.

## Game Boy Advance (5)

Doom, Grand Theft Auto Advance, LEGO Star Wars: The Video Game, Pokémon Emerald (Pokemon Emerald) and Super Mario Advance.

## Game Boy Color (4)

Grand Theft Auto, Grand Theft Auto 2, Pokémon Crystal (Pokemon Crystal) and Super Mario Bros. Deluxe.

## Game Boy (3)

Pokémon Red (Pokemon Red), Pokémon Yellow (Pokemon Yellow) and Space Invaders.

Web Retro also plays ROM files you bring yourself, from your computer or from proton Files, on the Emulators tab.
