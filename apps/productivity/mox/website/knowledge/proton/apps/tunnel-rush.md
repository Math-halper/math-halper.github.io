# Tunnel Rush

Tunnel Rush is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Tunnel Rush - dodge through the spinning tunnel."

Tunnel Rush is a fast 3D reflex game. You speed through a spinning tunnel; press the left and right arrow keys (or A and D) to rotate and dodge the obstacles coming at you.

Where Tunnel Rush comes from: The game files ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Tunnel Rush, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Tunnel Rush under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
