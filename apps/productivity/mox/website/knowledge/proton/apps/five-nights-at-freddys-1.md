# Five Nights at Freddy's

Five Nights at Freddy's is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Five Nights at Freddy's - survive the night shift at Freddy Fazbear’s."

Five Nights at Freddy's is the horror game by Scott Cawthon. You are the night guard at Freddy Fazbear's Pizza and must survive from 12 AM to 6 AM while the animatronic characters wander the building. Watch them on the security cameras, and close the two doors or switch on the hall lights when they come near, but every door, light and camera uses power, and the power runs out. Survive five nights.

Where Five Nights at Freddy's comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Five Nights at Freddy's, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Five Nights at Freddy's under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
