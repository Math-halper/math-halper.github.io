# Slope

Slope is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Slope - roll the ball down the neon slope."

Slope is a fast 3D running game. Steer a ball rolling down an endless neon slope with the left and right arrow keys (or A and D), stay on the track and avoid the red blocks. The ball speeds up the longer you survive.

Where Slope comes from: The game page ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Slope, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Slope under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
