# Bloons Tower Defense 1

Bloons Tower Defense 1 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Bloons Tower Defense 1 - the original balloon-popping tower defense."

Bloons Tower Defense 1 is the original Flash tower defence game by Ninja Kiwi. Balloons called bloons float along a track; buy towers such as dart monkeys and place them beside the track to pop the bloons before they reach the end. Popping bloons earns money for more towers, and every bloon that gets through costs lives. Play with the mouse. It runs on proton's Ruffle Flash Player, so no Flash plug-in is needed.

Where Bloons Tower Defense 1 comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Bloons Tower Defense 1, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Bloons Tower Defense 1 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
