# BitLife

BitLife is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play BitLife - Life Simulator in your browser."

BitLife is a life simulator. Live a whole life from birth: press Age to move forward a year, make choices about school, jobs, relationships, money and risky adventures, and see how your story turns out. Progress saves in this browser.

Where BitLife comes from: The game build ships with this app - copied from Lugapps-games/bitlife.

To open BitLife, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose BitLife under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
