# Bloons Tower Defense 5

Bloons Tower Defense 5 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Bloons Tower Defense 5 - the classic tower defense."

Bloons Tower Defense 5 is the classic Ninja Kiwi tower defence game: place monkeys and other towers along the track, upgrade them along two upgrade paths, and pop wave after wave of bloons, including tough ones such as lead and ceramic bloons and the giant blimps. Play with the mouse. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Bloons Tower Defense 5 comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Bloons Tower Defense 5, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Bloons Tower Defense 5 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
