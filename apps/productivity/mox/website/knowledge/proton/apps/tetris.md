# Tetris

Tetris is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Tetris - the falling-blocks classic."

Tetris is the falling-blocks classic as a Flash game. Move and rotate the falling pieces with the arrow keys so they fill complete rows; full rows disappear and score points. The game ends when the stack reaches the top. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Tetris comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Tetris, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Tetris under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
