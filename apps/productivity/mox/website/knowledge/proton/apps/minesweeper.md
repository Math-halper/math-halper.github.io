# Minesweeper

Minesweeper is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Minesweeper - the classic mine-hunting puzzler."

Minesweeper is the classic mine-hunting puzzle. Left-click a square to reveal it; a number shows how many mines touch that square. Right-click to put a flag on a square you think hides a mine. Reveal every safe square without clicking a mine to win.

Where Minesweeper comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Minesweeper, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Minesweeper under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
