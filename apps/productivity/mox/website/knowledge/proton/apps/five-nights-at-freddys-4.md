# Five Nights at Freddy's 4

Five Nights at Freddy's 4 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Five Nights at Freddy's 4 - the nightmare comes home."

Five Nights at Freddy's 4 moves the horror into a child's bedroom. Nightmare versions of the animatronics creep towards you; listen for breathing at the two doors, hold the doors shut when they are there, shine the flashlight down the halls, and check the closet and the bed behind you. Sound is the most important clue, so play with the audio on.

Where Five Nights at Freddy's 4 comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Five Nights at Freddy's 4, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Five Nights at Freddy's 4 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
