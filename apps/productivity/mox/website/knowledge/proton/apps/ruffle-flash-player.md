# Ruffle Flash Player

Ruffle Flash Player is one of the apps in the Tools section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Load and play your own Flash .swf files."

Ruffle Flash Player plays Adobe Flash games and animations (.swf files) without the old Flash plug-in. Ruffle is an open-source Flash emulator written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly. Inside the app, Local SWF at the top lets you choose a .swf file from your computer to play. The bar has Reload and Open in new tab.

Ruffle is also the engine behind proton's Flash games: Bloons Tower Defense 1, Bloons Tower Defense 2, Bloons Tower Defense 5, Bloxorz, Pacman, Tetris, The Impossible Quiz, Breaking The Bank, Escaping The Prison, Stealing The Diamond, Infiltrating The Airship and Fleeing The Complex load their Flash files through this app.

Where Ruffle Flash Player comes from: The Ruffle Flash player ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams (Ruffle is open source, MIT/Apache-2.0). Load any .swf you own.

To open Ruffle Flash Player, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Ruffle Flash Player under Tools, or type its name into the menu's search box.
