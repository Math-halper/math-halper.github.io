# You Are An Idiot

You Are An Idiot is one of the apps in the Tools section of the start menu. Its menu description: "The classic you-are-an-idiot desktop menace, quarantined inside proton: bouncing windows, flashing faces and that song. Not preinstalled - add it from App Center if you dare."

You Are An Idiot is not preinstalled. To get it, open App Center, find You Are An Idiot and click Install; it then appears in the start menu under Tools.

You Are An Idiot is a joke app: the classic "you are an idiot" prank, safely shut inside proton. It plays the looping video with the famous song, shakes the desktop and fills it with extra windows that bounce around on their own. Closing one with × only makes two more. The only way out is the Make it stop button that every one of its windows carries, which closes them all at once. Nothing leaves the proton desktop and nothing goes fullscreen.

To get rid of it for good, remove it again in App Center.

To open You Are An Idiot, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose You Are An Idiot under Tools, or type its name into the menu's search box.
