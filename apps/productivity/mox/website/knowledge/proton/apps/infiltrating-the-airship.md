# Infiltrating The Airship

Infiltrating The Airship is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Infiltrating The Airship - a Henry Stickmin adventure."

Infiltrating The Airship is the fourth Henry Stickmin game by PuffballsUnited. Henry is asked to infiltrate the airship of the Toppat Clan, a gang of thieves in top hats, and can side with the government or with the Toppats. Click the options at each decision; there are several paths and endings. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Infiltrating The Airship comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Infiltrating The Airship, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Infiltrating The Airship under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
