# Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Cookie Clicker - Orteil's idle bakery game, an offline mirror."

Cookie Clicker is Orteil's idle game. Click the big cookie to bake cookies, then spend them on buildings, such as cursors, grandmas, farms, mines and factories, that bake cookies for you, and on upgrades that make everything faster. The numbers grow enormous. Progress saves in this browser. This copy is an offline mirror of the game.

Where Cookie Clicker comes from: The game files ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams. Cookie Clicker is by Orteil (DashNet); this is an offline mirror of the browser game.

To open Cookie Clicker, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Cookie Clicker under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
