# Drive Mad

Drive Mad is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Drive Mad - the physics driving game."

Drive Mad is a physics driving game. Get your vehicle over hills, bridges, ramps and wobbly obstacles to the finish without flipping it or breaking it. Drive forward and back with the keyboard, and take it easy on jumps, because the car's balance matters. Each level brings a new contraption.

Where Drive Mad comes from: The game files ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Drive Mad, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Drive Mad under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
