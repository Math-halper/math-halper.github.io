# Baldi's Basics

Baldi's Basics is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Baldi's Basics - the surreal schoolhouse escape."

Baldi's Basics is a surreal horror game dressed up as an old educational game. Walk through the schoolhouse with W, A, S and D and look around with the mouse, collect the seven notebooks and answer their maths problems. Get one wrong and Baldi starts chasing you, faster with every notebook. Find all seven, then escape through an exit.

Where Baldi's Basics comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Baldi's Basics, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Baldi's Basics under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
