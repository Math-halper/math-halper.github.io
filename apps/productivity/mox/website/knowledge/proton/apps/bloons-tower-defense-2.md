# Bloons Tower Defense 2

Bloons Tower Defense 2 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Bloons Tower Defense 2 - more balloons, more towers."

Bloons Tower Defense 2 is Ninja Kiwi's second Flash tower defence game: more kinds of bloons, more towers and upgrades. Place monkey towers along the track with the mouse to pop every bloon before it escapes, and spend the money you earn on new towers and upgrades. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Bloons Tower Defense 2 comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Bloons Tower Defense 2, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Bloons Tower Defense 2 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
