# Super Mario 64

Super Mario 64 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play the Super Mario 64 WebAssembly browser build - unofficial fan port."

Super Mario 64 is an unofficial fan build of Nintendo's 1996 game for the browser, compiled to WebAssembly from the decompiled game. Explore Princess Peach's castle, jump into its paintings and collect Power Stars. Play with the keyboard or a gamepad. It is not affiliated with Nintendo.

Where Super Mario 64 comes from: The game files ship with this app — copied verbatim from Math-halper/Mario. Unofficial fan build; not affiliated with Nintendo.

To open Super Mario 64, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Super Mario 64 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
