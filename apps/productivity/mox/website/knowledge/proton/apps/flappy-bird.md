# Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Flappy Bird - tap to flap through the pipes."

Flappy Bird is the one-button game: click, tap or press the spacebar to flap and fly the bird through the gaps between the pipes. Every pipe passed scores a point; touching a pipe or the ground ends the run.

Where Flappy Bird comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Flappy Bird, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Flappy Bird under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
