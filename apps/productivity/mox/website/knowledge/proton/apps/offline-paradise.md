# Offline Paradise

Offline Paradise is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Offline Paradise."

Offline Paradise is a small single-file arcade game from the Gams-Offline/Gams collection. Press Space to jump and dodge the obstacles that come at you.

Where Offline Paradise comes from: The game page ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Offline Paradise, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Offline Paradise under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
