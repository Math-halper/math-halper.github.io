# Geometry Dash Lite

Geometry Dash Lite is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Geometry Dash Lite - jump and fly to the rhythm."

Geometry Dash Lite is the rhythm platformer by RobTop. Your square races forward on its own to the music; click, press the spacebar or press the up arrow to jump over spikes and gaps, and hold to fly when it turns into a ship. One mistake restarts the level, so learning the rhythm is the key.

Where Geometry Dash Lite comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Geometry Dash Lite, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Geometry Dash Lite under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
