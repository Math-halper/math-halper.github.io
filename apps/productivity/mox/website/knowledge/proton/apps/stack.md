# Stack

Stack is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Stack - time the falling blocks."

Stack is a timing game. A block slides back and forth over the tower; click, or press the spacebar, to drop it. Only the part that lands on the block below stays, so line them up exactly to keep the tower wide and build it as high as you can. Click or press the spacebar to start again.

Where Stack comes from: The game page ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Stack, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Stack under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
