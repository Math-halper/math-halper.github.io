# Fleeing The Complex

Fleeing The Complex is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Fleeing The Complex - a Henry Stickmin adventure."

Fleeing The Complex is the fifth Henry Stickmin game by PuffballsUnited. Henry is locked in the Wall, a high-security prison complex, and must find a way out, possibly with the help of other characters. Click the options at each decision; it has many paths and endings. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Fleeing The Complex comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Fleeing The Complex, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Fleeing The Complex under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
