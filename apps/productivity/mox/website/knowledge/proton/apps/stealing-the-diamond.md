# Stealing The Diamond

Stealing The Diamond is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Stealing The Diamond - a Henry Stickmin adventure."

Stealing The Diamond is the third Henry Stickmin game by PuffballsUnited. Henry tries to steal the famous Tunisian Diamond from a museum. Click the options at each decision; most fail in funny ways, and the right ones lead to the diamond and several endings. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Stealing The Diamond comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Stealing The Diamond, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Stealing The Diamond under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
