# Drift Boss

Drift Boss is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Drift Boss - one-button drifting on a floating track."

Drift Boss is a one-button driving game. A car drives along a zigzag track floating in the sky; press and hold the mouse button or a key to turn, and release to go straight. Drift around each corner without falling off the edge to score.

Where Drift Boss comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Drift Boss, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Drift Boss under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
