# Celeste Hub

Celeste Hub is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "All the Celeste Classic games in one place - choose which one to play."

The Celeste Hub collects every Celeste Classic game in one app. It opens on a list of games; click one to play it, and Change game in the bar brings the list back. Celeste Classic is the PICO-8 mountain-climbing platformer by Maddy Thorson and Noel Berry that grew into the full game Celeste: use the arrow keys to move, C or Z to jump and X to dash, and climb the mountain one screen at a time. Celeste Classic 2: Lani's Trek is its sequel, with a grappling hook.

The Celeste Hub lists 8 pages to choose from:
- **Celeste Classic**: Play the original Celeste Classic - its actual game files, vendored.
- **Celeste Classic 2**: Play Celeste Classic 2: Lani's Trek - its actual game files, vendored.
- **Celeste Community**: Celeste Classic's community hub - practice mods, downloads and links.
- **Celeste Classic Practice Mod**: Play the Celeste Classic practice mod.
- **Celeste Classic 2 Practice Mod 1.0**: Play the Celeste Classic 2 v1.0 practice mod.
- **Celeste Classic 2 Practice Mod 1.1**: Play the Celeste Classic 2 v1.1 practice mod.
- **Celeste Webek**: Play Webek - a Celeste Classic PICO-8 game.
- **Celeste Classic Downloads**: Celeste Classic downloads - standalone builds and links.

Where Celeste Hub comes from: Every Celeste game ships with this app — copied verbatim from Math-halper/Celeste. Change game brings the list back.

To open Celeste Hub, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Celeste Hub under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
