# Granny

Granny is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Granny - escape the house quietly before she hears you."

Granny is a first-person horror escape game. You wake up locked in Granny's house and have five days to find the tools and keys you need to escape. Move with the keyboard and look with the mouse, and stay quiet: Granny hears every noise and comes after you. It is a large game and takes a while to load the first time.

Where Granny comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Granny, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Granny under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
