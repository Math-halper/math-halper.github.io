# Stickman Hook

Stickman Hook is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Stickman Hook - swing through the levels."

Stickman Hook is a swinging game. Press and hold the mouse button, or the spacebar, to grab the nearest hook with a rope and swing; let go to fly through the air, then grab the next hook. Reach the finish of each level without falling.

Where Stickman Hook comes from: The game page ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Stickman Hook, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Stickman Hook under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
