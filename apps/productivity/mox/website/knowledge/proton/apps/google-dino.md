# Google Dino

Google Dino is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Google Dino - jump and duck through the endless desert runner."

Google Dino is the endless runner from Chrome's no-internet page. Press the spacebar or the up arrow to jump over cacti and the down arrow to duck under birds. The dinosaur runs faster as the score climbs.

Where Google Dino comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Google Dino, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Google Dino under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
