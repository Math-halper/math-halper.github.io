# Backrooms

Backrooms is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Backrooms - wander the endless yellow rooms."

Backrooms is a first-person horror exploration game set in the endless yellow rooms of the Backrooms legend. Walk with the keyboard, look around with the mouse, and try to find your way through the maze while something hunts you. It is a large game and takes a while to load the first time.

Where Backrooms comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Backrooms, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Backrooms under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
