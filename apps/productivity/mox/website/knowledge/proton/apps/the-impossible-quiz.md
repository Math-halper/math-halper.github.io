# The Impossible Quiz

The Impossible Quiz is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play The Impossible Quiz - the wildly unfair quiz."

The Impossible Quiz is a famously unfair Flash quiz by Splapp-Me-Do. Click the answer to each question, but many questions are trick questions with answers hidden in odd places. Wrong answers cost lives, and some questions are bombs with a timer. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where The Impossible Quiz comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open The Impossible Quiz, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose The Impossible Quiz under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
