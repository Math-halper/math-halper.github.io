# Escaping The Prison

Escaping The Prison is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Escaping The Prison - a Henry Stickmin adventure."

Escaping The Prison is the second Henry Stickmin game by PuffballsUnited. Henry has been locked up, and a friend sends him a cake with a choice of hidden tools. Click the options to choose tools and actions; most choices fail in funny ways, and the right ones lead to several different escapes. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Escaping The Prison comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Escaping The Prison, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Escaping The Prison under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
