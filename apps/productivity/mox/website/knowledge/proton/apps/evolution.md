# Evolution

Evolution is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Evolution - build creatures from joints, bones and muscles and evolve their brains."

Evolution is a web version of Keiwan Donyagard's evolution simulator. Build a creature from joints, bones and muscles, choose a task such as running, jumping or climbing, and watch a neural network learn to move it, generation after generation, through an evolutionary algorithm. It shows how machine learning can teach creatures to walk.

Where Evolution comes from: The game ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/evo.

To open Evolution, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Evolution under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
