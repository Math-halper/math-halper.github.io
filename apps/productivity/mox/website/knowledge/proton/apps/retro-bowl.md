# Retro Bowl

Retro Bowl is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Retro Bowl - retro American football management."

Retro Bowl is a retro-style American football game that mixes playing the matches with running the team. In a match you control the offence: drag back to aim a pass and release to throw, or run with the ball carrier. Between games you manage the roster, hire and fire players and keep the fans and staff happy across seasons. Progress saves in this browser.

Where Retro Bowl comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Retro Bowl, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Retro Bowl under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
