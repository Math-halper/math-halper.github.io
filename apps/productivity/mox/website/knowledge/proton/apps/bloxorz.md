# Bloxorz

Bloxorz is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Bloxorz - roll the block to the goal."

Bloxorz is a Flash puzzle game. Roll a tall block across a floating platform with the arrow keys and drop it standing upright into the square hole to finish each level. Do not let it fall off the edge. Orange tiles collapse if the block stands on them upright, switches open and close bridges, and in some levels the block splits in two, with Space switching between the halves. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Bloxorz comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Bloxorz, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Bloxorz under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
