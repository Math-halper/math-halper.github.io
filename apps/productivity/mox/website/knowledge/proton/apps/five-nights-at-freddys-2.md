# Five Nights at Freddy's 2

Five Nights at Freddy's 2 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Five Nights at Freddy's 2 - new animatronics, no doors."

Five Nights at Freddy's 2 has no doors to close. Survive the night shift with the security cameras, a flashlight to check the halls and vents, and an empty Freddy Fazbear mask to fool most animatronics. Keep the music box wound up from the cameras, or the Puppet comes for you.

Where Five Nights at Freddy's 2 comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Five Nights at Freddy's 2, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Five Nights at Freddy's 2 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
