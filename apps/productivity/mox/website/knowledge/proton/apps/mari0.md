# Mari0

Mari0 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Mari0 - Mario x Portal (Stabyourself), the love.js browser build."

Mari0 is Mario crossed with Portal, by Maurice Guégan and Stabyourself.net: the levels of Super Mario Bros. with a portal gun. Move and jump with the keyboard, aim with the mouse, and click the left and right mouse buttons to shoot the two portals, then jump through them to cross the level in new ways. This is the love.js browser build.

Where Mari0 comes from: The game files ship with this app — copied verbatim from Math-halper/Mario. Mari0 is by Maurice Guégan / Stabyourself (MIT); the browser runtime is love.js (MIT — see the notices in this folder).

To open Mari0, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Mari0 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
