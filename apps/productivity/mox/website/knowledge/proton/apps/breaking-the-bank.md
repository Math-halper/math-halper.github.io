# Breaking The Bank

Breaking The Bank is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Breaking The Bank - a Henry Stickmin adventure."

Breaking The Bank is the first Henry Stickmin game by PuffballsUnited, a funny Flash point-and-click adventure. Henry wants to get into a bank vault: at each decision you click one of several options, and most choices end in a silly failure, after which you can try again. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Breaking The Bank comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Breaking The Bank, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Breaking The Bank under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
