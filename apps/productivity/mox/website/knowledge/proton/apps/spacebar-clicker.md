# Spacebar Clicker

Spacebar Clicker is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Spacebar Clicker - mash the spacebar."

Spacebar Clicker is an idle clicker game: press the spacebar to earn points, then spend them on helpers, such as a monkey and a boomer mom, that press it for you. Keep upgrading to make the numbers grow faster.

Where Spacebar Clicker comes from: The game page ships with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams.

To open Spacebar Clicker, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Spacebar Clicker under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
