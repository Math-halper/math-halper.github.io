# Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location

Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location - a darker shift underground."

Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location takes the series underground to Circus Baby's Entertainment and Rental. Each night you do different jobs as a technician, guided by a voice on the intercom: crawl through vents, keep animatronics such as Ballora and Funtime Foxy away, and follow instructions carefully, because breaking the rules is deadly.

Where Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
