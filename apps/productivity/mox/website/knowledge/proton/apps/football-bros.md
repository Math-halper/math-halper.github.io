# Football Bros

Football Bros is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Football Bros - chaotic physics football."

Football Bros is a chaotic physics American football game. Run, pass, juke and tackle with cartoon players in quick matches. The game shows its own keyboard controls. It is a large game and takes a while to load the first time.

Where Football Bros comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Football Bros, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Football Bros under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
