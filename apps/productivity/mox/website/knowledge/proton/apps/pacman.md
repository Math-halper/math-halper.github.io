# Pacman

Pacman is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Pacman - the arcade classic."

Pacman is the arcade classic as a Flash game. Steer Pac-Man around the maze with the arrow keys, eat every dot and avoid the ghosts. Eating a power pellet turns the ghosts blue for a short time so you can eat them. It runs on the Ruffle Flash Player.

Where Pacman comes from: The game's Flash build and its loader shim ship with this app — copied verbatim from Gams-Offline/Gams; the shim's loader paths point at the shared Ruffle Flash Player app (in Tools). The game is © its respective authors.

To open Pacman, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Pacman under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
