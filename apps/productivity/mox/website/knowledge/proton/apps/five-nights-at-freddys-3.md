# Five Nights at Freddy's 3

Five Nights at Freddy's 3 is one of the games in the Games section of the start menu. Its menu description: "Play Five Nights at Freddy's 3 - one animatronic, thirty years later."

Five Nights at Freddy's 3 is set thirty years later in Fazbear's Fright, a horror attraction with one real animatronic, Springtrap. Track him on the cameras, lure him away with audio, seal vents, and reboot the camera, audio and ventilation systems from the maintenance panel when they fail.

Where Five Nights at Freddy's 3 comes from: The game page ships with this app - copied verbatim from proton-arcade/proton-arcade.github.io.

To open Five Nights at Freddy's 3, click the menu button with the atom logo at the left end of the panel and choose Five Nights at Freddy's 3 under Games, or type its name into the menu's search box.
