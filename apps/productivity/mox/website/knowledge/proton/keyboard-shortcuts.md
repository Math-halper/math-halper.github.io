# Keyboard shortcuts on proton

The window in front takes the keyboard, so each app's shortcuts work as soon as you click its window or open it. Shortcuts never fire while you are typing in a text box unless they say so.

## Desktop-wide keyboard shortcuts (Alt+C)

- **Alt+C** (hold Alt and press C): open or close the AI pane with Mox, from anywhere on the desktop, inside Mox too.
- **Left and right arrow keys** on the AI pane's left edge (after clicking it): resize the pane; hold Shift for bigger steps.

## Desktop shortcut keys

With a desktop shortcut selected: Enter or Space opens it, Alt with an arrow key moves it, Delete or Backspace removes it, and Shift+F10 or the Menu key opens its right-click menu.

## Files keyboard shortcuts

In Files: Alt+Up goes up one folder, the up and down arrows move through the list, Enter opens the selected item, Space selects it, F2 renames it, Delete deletes it and the Menu key opens its right-click menu. Enter in the path box goes to that folder, and Enter in the search box searches.

## Shortcuts in the other apps

- **Text Editor**: Ctrl+S (Cmd+S on a Mac) saves.
- **Image Viewer**: left and right arrows show the previous and next picture in the folder, + and - zoom, 0 fits the picture to the window and 1 shows its actual size.
- **Browser**: Alt+Left and Alt+Right go back and forward, Alt+Home goes to the start page. In the address bar, the up and down arrows pick a suggestion, Enter goes and Escape closes the suggestions.
- **Calculator**: digits and . type numbers, + - * / are the operators, Enter is equals, Backspace deletes a digit, and Esc or C clears.
- **Piano**: the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P and ; play the notes from middle C upwards.
- **Terminal**: the up and down arrows bring back earlier commands. In nano, Ctrl+S saves and Ctrl+X exits.
- **VLC**: the desktop VLC hotkeys: Space play or pause, S stop, N and P next and previous, F fullscreen, M mute, Ctrl+Up and Ctrl+Down volume, Shift, Alt or Ctrl with Left and Right to seek, + and - for speed, Alt+Enter to open the file in a new browser tab, and Help > Keyboard shortcuts in VLC for the full list.

## Keyboard in games

Games run in a frame inside their window. Click the game once so it gets the keyboard; after that its own controls work. Each game's page in this library lists its controls.
