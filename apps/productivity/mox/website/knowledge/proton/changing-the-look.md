# Changing the look: wallpaper, accent color, themes and icons

proton is black and white by design: dark windows, white text and gray accents. You can still change a lot of how it looks, per account.

## Changing the wallpaper

Open Settings and pick a wallpaper under Wallpaper: Mint, Ocean, Sunset, Grape or Slate, all gray gradients, or any wallpaper you have made. To make your own, open Customize, go to Wallpapers, give it a name and two colors, and press "Add & apply wallpaper". Your wallpapers then appear in Settings too.

## Changing the accent color

The accent color is used for primary buttons, highlights and the underline of the app in front. Settings > Accent colour offers White, Silver, Light grey, Grey and Dark grey: white and four shades of gray. In Customize > Themes, the built-in proton Dark theme has the same accent choices.

## Themes

A theme is an accent color plus a wallpaper, applied together in one click. Customize > Themes shows the built-in proton Dark theme and lets you add your own: give it a name, pick an accent color (any color) and a wallpaper, and press "Add & apply theme". Your themes can be applied, edited and deleted later.

## Renaming apps and changing their icons

To rename an app or change its icon, use Customize > App modules, which has a card for every app. Change its name, icon, menu section (Category), position in its section (Order) or description, then press Save changes; Reset undoes your changes to that app. An icon can be one of proton's built-in vector icons or a picture you upload (up to 8 MB), which is shrunk and saved with your account.

## Desktop parts

Customize > Desktop parts switches parts of the desktop on and off instantly: the clock, the date and hint on the desktop, the lock button, the account button, the search box in the proton menu, names next to the panel icons, and the AI button. The same section has Add application icon and Arrange icons.

## Starting over

Customize > Start over > Reset customisations puts every customization back to proton's defaults: names, icons, removed apps, pins, themes, wallpapers, desktop parts and the desktop shortcut layout. Your files and other content are not touched.
