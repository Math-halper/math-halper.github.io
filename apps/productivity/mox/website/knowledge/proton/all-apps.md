# Apps on this website

The full list of all the apps on proton, the desktop on this website. It has 65 apps in all: 20 apps (6 in System, 6 in Productivity, 4 in Media, 4 in Tools) and 45 games in the Games section (see "Games on this website"). Open any of them from the proton menu, the button at the left end of the panel.

## System apps

- **App Center**: Manage the built-in apps and any saved apps or websites on this account.
- **Customize**: Remove & edit apps, themes, wallpapers and desktop parts.
- **Settings**: Account, wallpaper, backups and this device.
- **About**: What this is, credits and how saving works.
- **Browser**: Tabs, back/forward, bookmarks and history — and a search bar that answers from proton's own index of this repository.
- **System Monitor**: Live CPU and memory graphs, open windows and storage.

## Productivity apps

- **Calculator**: Do quick maths from the keypad or your keyboard.
- **Clock**: The time and date, a stopwatch and a countdown timer.
- **Files**: Browse your home folder and the read-only proton system tree - create, rename, move, import, search and download.
- **Text Editor**: Open, edit and save text files in your home folder; proton's own files open read-only.
- **PDF Viewer**: Read PDF files in your browser's own viewer - no extra library, nothing uploaded.
- **Mox**: Ask a private AI that runs in your browser - a bundled language model, a cited knowledge library, a code workspace and a calculator.

## Media apps

- **Image Viewer**: View PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, BMP, ICO and SVG files with zoom and a folder playlist.
- **VLC**: VLC media player — the open-source libvlc engine compiled to WebAssembly; plays audio and video itself.
- **Paint**: Draw on a canvas and save PNGs into your Pictures folder.
- **Piano**: Play two octaves, record what you play and hear it back.

## Tools apps

- **Ruffle Flash Player**: Load and play your own Flash .swf files.
- **Web Retro**: Play retro classics or your own ROMs - N64, SNES, NES, Game Boy.
- **Terminal**: A small shell over your proton Files: ls, cd, cat, open.
- **You Are An Idiot**: The classic you-are-an-idiot desktop menace, quarantined inside proton: bouncing windows, flashing faces and that song. Not preinstalled - add it from App Center if you dare. (not preinstalled: install it from App Center)
