# Saving, backups and privacy on proton

proton has no server and no cloud. Everything you do is saved automatically in this browser on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## What proton saves, and where

Each account's desktop shortcuts, pinned apps, customizations, themes, wallpapers, files and folders, bookmarks and browsing history in the Browser, and the AI pane's width are saved in this browser's local storage as you work. Mox keeps its own chats, settings and added files in this browser's IndexedDB storage. The Math Helper cover page keeps its practice progress separately on the same device.

## Backing up your proton account

Settings > Backup has two buttons. "Export backup (.json)…" saves everything for this account as one file, either to your computer or into proton Files. "Import backup…" restores a backup file and brings your desktop back, on this browser or another one. Export a backup before clearing browser data, changing browsers or moving to another device.

## Clearing browser data deletes proton

Because proton saves only in the browser, clearing this site's data (cookies and site data, or local storage) deletes every account and file on this device. Private or incognito windows forget everything when they close. A backup is the only way to keep a copy.

## Privacy on proton

proton does not send your files, typing or searches to any server: the Browser's search answers from an index built into the website, and Mox's AI model runs on your own device. Account passwords are stored as salted SHA-256 hashes, never as plain text. The email on an account is optional and never leaves the device. Web pages you visit at outside addresses in the Browser are loaded from those sites, as in any browser.

## Deleting your proton account

Settings > Danger zone > "Delete my account & all its data" deletes the current account and everything it saved on this device. It cannot be undone.
