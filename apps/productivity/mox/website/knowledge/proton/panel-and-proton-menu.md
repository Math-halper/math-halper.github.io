# The panel (taskbar) and the proton menu (start menu)

The panel is the taskbar: the dark bar along the bottom of the screen. From left to right it holds the menu button, the app buttons, and the tray with the padlock, your account button, the clock and the AI button.

## The proton menu button

The button at the left end of the panel, an atom logo with the word proton, opens the proton menu, proton's start menu. Click it again to close the menu. To open an app, click this button, then click the app in the menu; apps on the desktop open with a double-click.

## Inside the proton menu

The proton menu has a search box at the top ("Search apps…") that filters the apps by name and description as you type. Below it, the apps are grouped into sections in this order: System, Productivity, Media, Tools, Games, then My Apps and any section you create in Customize. Click an app to open it, drag it onto the wallpaper to make a desktop shortcut, or right-click it to add it to the desktop. The bottom of the menu shows your account (initials, display name and @username) with a Lock button and a Sign out button.

## App buttons on the panel

The middle of the panel shows one button for each pinned app and each running app. By default Settings, App Center and Browser are pinned. Each button shows the app's icon, with its name on hover (Customize > Desktop parts > Names next to panel icons shows the names all the time). An underline marks a running app and a brighter one the app in front. Clicking the button of an app that is not running opens it; clicking a running app brings it to the front; clicking the app already in front minimizes it. Pin or unpin an app in App Center or Customize.

## The tray at the right end of the panel

- **Padlock**: locks the desktop (unlocking asks for the desktop password).
- **Account button** with your initials: opens Settings.
- **Clock**: the time over a short date, updated every 15 seconds.
- **AI button**: the sparkle at the far right, after the clock, where Windows 11 keeps its Copilot button. It opens Mox, the desktop's AI, in a pane on the right; Alt+C does the same. See "The AI button (proton's Copilot) and Mox, the desktop's AI".

Every one of these parts can be hidden or shown again in Customize > Desktop parts: the clock, the date and hint, the lock button, the account button, the menu search box, the names next to panel icons and the AI button.
