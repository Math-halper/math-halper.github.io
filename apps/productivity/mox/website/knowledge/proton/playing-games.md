# Playing games on proton

proton has 45 games in the Games section of the proton menu, and Web Retro in Tools adds 34 classic console games. Every game's files are stored on this website itself, copied from the projects that made them, so nothing is downloaded from other sites while you play. "Games on this website" lists them all, and every game has its own page in this library with how to play it.

## Starting a game on proton

Open the proton menu, scroll to Games (or type the game's name in the menu search box) and click the game. It opens in a window. Some games, such as Kart Bros, Granny, Backrooms and Football Bros, are large files and take a while to load the first time.

## Game window controls

Every game window has a bar with the game's name, Reload (restart the game) and Open in new tab (play it in a browser tab of its own, which can be better for big games). Maximize the window, or use the game's own fullscreen button, for a bigger view. Click the game once so it gets the keyboard; until then, key presses go to the desktop.

## Saving game progress

Games that save, such as Cookie Clicker, BitLife, Bitburner and Retro Bowl, keep their progress in this browser's storage for this website. Clearing the browser's data resets them. Progress is per browser, not per proton account.

## Kinds of games on proton

- **Flash classics**, played with the Ruffle Flash Player: Bloons Tower Defense 1, Bloons Tower Defense 2, Bloons Tower Defense 5, Bloxorz, Pacman, Tetris and The Impossible Quiz.
- **The Henry Stickmin adventures**, also Flash: Breaking The Bank, Escaping The Prison, Stealing The Diamond, Infiltrating The Airship and Fleeing The Complex.
- **Arcade and one-button games**: 2048, Slope, Tunnel Rush, Stack, Stickman Hook, Spacebar Clicker, Offline Paradise, Flappy Bird, Google Dino, Drift Boss, Geometry Dash Lite and Minesweeper.
- **Horror**: Five Nights at Freddy's, Five Nights at Freddy's 2, Five Nights at Freddy's 3, Five Nights at Freddy's 4, Five Nights at Freddy's: Sister Location, Granny, Backrooms and Baldi's Basics.
- **Sports and driving**: Retro Bowl, Football Bros, Kart Bros and Drive Mad.
- **Mario and Celeste**: Mari0, Super Mario 64 and Celeste Hub, which holds every Celeste Classic game.
- **Simulation, strategy and more**: Cookie Clicker, BitLife, Bitburner, Evolution and Grey Box Testing.
- **Minecraft**: Ampler Launcher, with seven offline Eaglercraft builds.

## The games picker page

Outside the desktop, the page apps/games/index.html lists every game as a plain link with a one-line description, for browsing the game folders directly.

## Sound in games

Browsers only allow a page to play sound after you click it, so a game may stay silent until you click inside it.
