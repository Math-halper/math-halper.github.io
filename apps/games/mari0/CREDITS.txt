## Mari0 — Mario × Portal

- Author: **Maurice Guégan / Stabyourself**, with additional contributors credited in the game's own source and menus.
- Source: <https://github.com/Stabyourself/mari0>, commit `57829fd23e783d1a2993b9d64a7f7e6b131e572f` (v1.6 / LÖVE 11.4).
- Upstream README and `main.lua` state the **MIT** license; no standalone MIT text ships upstream, so none is invented here.
- Browser runtime: [**love.js**](https://github.com/Davidobot/love.js) by **Davidobot**, continuing **Tanner Rogalsky's** original Emscripten port of LÖVE (both MIT). Its license is bundled at `mari0/web/LOVEJS-LICENSE.txt`.
- Browser-specific changes: LuaSocket replaced by offline stubs, a single-threaded streaming music loader, GLSL ported to WebGL ES 1.0 rules, save-folder opening disabled, online update/mappack/netplay paths turned off, LÖVE's stop-all audio call replaced by a per-source sweep (love.js's OpenAL shim crashes on it, which killed the game on every death), and portal input rebuilt because upstream only fired portals from the mouse and looked the binding up with the wrong index (**Q** switches the held colour, and any click or tap fires it, so touch works too).
- Mario, Portal, and other third-party artwork, music, and trademarks may carry separate rights. A code license is not blanket asset clearance.
