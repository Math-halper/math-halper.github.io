# The Math Helper cover page

Math Helper is the maths practice site that opens first on this website, before proton. It is a complete practice site for Pre-K to Year 12 that runs entirely in the browser, and every part of it works. Its progress is saved only on this device.

## What is on the Math Helper page

- **Header**: the Math Helper logo (back to the top), the menu Learning, Diagnostic, Analytics and Membership, a skill search bar, and the Sign in button that leads to proton.
- **Hero**: "Mathematics · Pre-K to Year 12" with Start practicing and Take a diagnostic buttons.
- **Today for you**: two recommended skills with progress bars and a Resume practice button. Recommendations update as you practice.
- **Explore by grade**: buttons for every grade from Pre-K to 12, each with its skill count, plus See all grades.
- **Popular maths skills**: cards for Fractions, Multiplication, Algebra, Geometry, Decimals and Statistics, each with a progress bar and Start skill.
- **Diagnostic**: "Find exactly what to practice next", with Start diagnostic.
- **Made for families and classrooms**, the **waitlist** band and a footer with About, Help centre, Membership and Privacy links.

## Practicing on Math Helper

Search for a skill, pick a grade or a skill card, and Math Helper generates practice questions. Choose an answer to get instant marking and an explanation. Each correct practice answer earns 10 learning points, and every skill tracks how many you got right and your mastery percentage. The page "Math Helper skills and grades" lists every skill.

## The Math Helper diagnostic

The diagnostic asks six questions across different skills and turns your answers into a personal action plan of what to practice next. It is saved, and it feeds the recommendations.

## Awards, analytics and membership

- **Awards**: First Steps (answer your first question), Getting Warm (5 correct), Sharp Solver (15 correct), Math Star (150 learning points), Skill Builder (practice 3 different skills), Mastery Maker (90% mastery in a skill) and Diagnostic Explorer (complete a diagnostic).
- **Analytics** shows your accuracy, learning points and growth by topic.
- **Membership** has three plans: Free ($0: the practice library, progress tracking and one diagnostic plan on this device), Family ($8 a month) and Classroom ($15 a month). Free is chosen instantly; Family and Classroom are joined through the waitlist.

## The Math Helper waitlist

The waitlist form asks for a name, an email and a plan, and it only adds you when the site password is entered correctly. Having a proton account does not put anyone on the waitlist. Entries are listed on this device with a remove option.

## Math Helper privacy

Practice progress, diagnostic plans, the membership choice and waitlist entries are stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing the browser's storage removes the learning record.

## Getting from Math Helper to proton

The only way from Math Helper into proton is the Sign in button in the header; see "Signing in and out of proton (log in, log out), accounts and locking".
