# Removing, restoring, renaming, pinning and installing apps

Every app and game on proton can be removed from the proton menu, added back, pinned to the panel and put on the desktop. These changes are per account and never delete anything from the website.

## Removing an app

Open App Center and press Remove on the app's card, then confirm. The app disappears from the proton menu, the panel and its desktop shortcuts, and its window closes. App Center and Settings cannot be removed, so you can never lock yourself out of managing apps. Customize > App modules has a Remove button on each card too.

## Adding an app back

Removed apps stay in App Center, dimmed and marked Removed. Press Add back to return the app to the proton menu.

## Installing You Are An Idiot

One app is not preinstalled: You Are An Idiot. Find it in App Center and press Install; it then appears in the Tools section of the proton menu. Remove it again in App Center.

## Renaming an app

To rename an app, open Customize, find the app under App modules, type a new Name and press Save changes. The new name shows in the proton menu, on the panel and on desktop shortcuts. Reset on the same card brings the original name back.

## Pinning apps to the panel

Press Pin on an app's card in App Center (or in Customize > App modules) to keep its button on the panel; press Unpin to take it off. Settings, App Center and Browser are pinned at first.

## Putting an app on the desktop

Press Desktop icon on the app's card in App Center, drag the app from the proton menu onto the wallpaper, or use Add icon on the desktop. See "The proton desktop and its shortcuts".

## Searching for an app

Type in the search box at the top of the proton menu, or in App Center's "Search apps" box, to find an app by its name or description.
