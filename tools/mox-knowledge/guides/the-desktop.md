# The proton desktop and its shortcuts

The desktop is the big area above the panel: a wallpaper, today's date with a hint in the top corner, and icons down the left side. A new account starts with four icons there: Settings, Browser, Files and Home, a link to your own home folder.

## Opening and moving desktop shortcuts

Double-click a desktop shortcut to open it. Click once to select it, then drag it to move it; dropping it onto another icon swaps the two. On a touch screen, tap an icon once and press Open selected. With the keyboard, Enter or Space opens the selected shortcut, Alt with an arrow key moves it, and Delete removes it.

## Adding desktop shortcuts

To add (create) a desktop shortcut for an app, press Add icon at the bottom right of the desktop and pick the app, drag the app from the proton menu onto the wallpaper, right-click the app in the proton menu, or press Desktop icon on its card in App Center. To link a file or folder, right-click the wallpaper and choose Add file or folder… (type a path or pick one from your home folder), right-click it in Files and choose Add desktop shortcut, or drag it out of Files onto the wallpaper. A desktop holds up to 200 shortcuts.

## Removing and arranging desktop shortcuts

Remove a shortcut with Remove selected, by right-clicking it and choosing Remove shortcut (keep original), or with the Delete key. A shortcut is only a link: removing it never deletes the app or the file. A file shortcut follows the file when it is renamed and is marked when the file goes missing. Right-click empty wallpaper and choose Arrange icons to tidy the grid. The layout is saved with your account, carried in Settings backups and reset by Customize > Reset customisations.

## The desktop right-click menu

Right-clicking the desktop opens a small menu. On a shortcut it offers Open and Remove shortcut (keep original). On empty wallpaper it offers Add application…, Add file or folder… and Arrange icons. Right-clicking an app in the proton menu offers Open and Add to desktop (or Remove from desktop).

## The wallpaper and the date hint

The wallpaper is a gray gradient you choose in Settings or Customize: Mint, Ocean, Sunset, Grape, Slate, or one you make yourself. The date and hint can be hidden in Customize > Desktop parts. See "Changing the look: wallpaper, accent color, themes and icons".
